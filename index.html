A morning email for UCLA dining. You name a few foods. At 7:00am Pacific, this checks the day’s menu and tells you where they are.

  1. Sign up with your email, the foods you want, and an optional backup preference.
  2. At 7:00am Pacific, read menus at a glance for today’s date in Los Angeles.
  3. Match your foods, including close names like bulgogi for beef. If you left a preference, add a few other dishes from that same menu.
  4. Email the halls and meals. Skip the send when the halls are closed.
  5. Unsubscribe from the link in the footer.
